  • Drag & drop a spreadsheet, photos or a saved project anywhere on the page.
  • Photos are matched to students by PhotoFileName, LRN, or Last_First file names.
  • Hover a card for photo tools; drag the photo to reposition and scroll to zoom.
  • Tick students in the roster to print, edit or delete them in bulk. Use 📍 to jump to a student's card.
  • Deletes, imports and edits can be undone. Everything autosaves in this browser — use Save Project for a backup file.
  • The preview is true size at 100% zoom. Print with Scale 100% and Margins: None.
